# Fortinet logo usage

Use official artwork from `assets/logos/`. Do not redraw, trace, recreate, distort, or typeset the Fortinet logo.

## Preferred logo

- The logo combines the Fortinet Grid logomark and Fortinet logotype.
- Preferred general usage is the logo **without** the registered trademark symbol.
- Use the registered-mark version only when a legal context specifically requires it; the bundled production assets intentionally use the preferred unregistered artwork.
- Primary full-color treatment: black logotype + Fortinet Red Grid on a light background.
- Negative full-color treatment: white logotype + Fortinet Red Grid on a dark background.
- All-white or all-black versions are fallback treatments only when the preferred color options do not have enough contrast.
- Maintain at least 3.0:1 contrast against the background.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Full logo clear space is based on the cap height of the logotype (the brand guide's `X`). Keep graphics, text, and image detail outside that area.
- Full-logo minimum width: 54 px digital; 0.75 in / 19 mm print.
- Grid clear space: 50% of the Grid height on each side.
- Grid minimum width: 17 px digital; 0.25 in / 6 mm print.

## Do not

- Rotate or manipulate the logo.
- Add shadows, outlines, glows, bevels, or other effects.
- Place it over a visually busy background.
- Use a background that reduces contrast below 3.0:1.
- Combine part of the logo with other words to invent an unauthorized lockup.
- Recolor the Grid or logotype outside the approved artwork treatments.

## Grid-only usage

The Grid may be used as a Fortinet shorthand only when the Fortinet brand is already established or where space is too constrained for the full logo, such as an application icon.

## Bundled production assets

PNG and SVG versions are supplied for:

- `fortinet-logo-rgb-black-red` - preferred light-background logo
- `fortinet-logo-rgb-white-red` - preferred dark-background logo
- `fortinet-logo-rgb-black` - exceptional monochrome light-background use
- `fortinet-logo-rgb-white` - exceptional monochrome dark-background use
- `fortinet-logomark-rgb-red` - Grid in Fortinet Red
- `fortinet-logomark-rgb-black` - exceptional monochrome Grid
- `fortinet-logomark-rgb-white` - reverse Grid

Use SVG when the target pipeline supports it reliably; otherwise use the PNG.
